Story 2: The Man and the Snake
[MAN_NAME] found the snake on a winter's evening,
coiled and nearly frozen on his doorstep. Against his better judgment, 
he brought [SNAKE_NAME] inside, warming the creature by his fire.
As the snake revived, [MAN_NAME] prepared a small enclosure.
"You'll be safe here until spring," 
he said, though [SNAKE_NAME] only watched him with unblinking eyes.
Weeks passed. [MAN_NAME] grew fond of his strange companion, 
speaking to [SNAKE_NAME] about his day, his worries, his dreams.
The snake would rest its head on [MAN_NAME]'s hand, 
neither affectionate nor hostile, simply present.
When spring arrived, [MAN_NAME] opened the door. 
[SNAKE_NAME] slithered to the threshold, paused, 
then disappeared into the greening world. 
[MAN_NAME]smiled. Some debts, he understood, 
are repaid not with loyalty but with the wisdom to know when to leave.
